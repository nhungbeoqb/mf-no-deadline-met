A single-line joke excusing a missed deadline, for the make.fun bounty
[One Line, No Deadline Met](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-no-deadline-met-6adc00ce).

`main.py` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I finished it on time in my head, ahead of schedule in my dreams, and three days late in the only timeline that counts — this one.

Three-beat escalation: on time in my head, ahead of schedule in my dreams,
three days late in the only timeline that counts. 130 characters, one line,
no setup — the excuse is the joke.
